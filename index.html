The Pipeline

How SamAadhan Works

A seamless, AI-driven process from problem identification to real-world solution deployment.

1. Submission

Citizens & local bodies submit issues via web/mobile with multimedia evidence and geo-tags.

2. AI Routing

AI automatically categorizes the problem domain and routes it to specialized university faculties.

3. R&D Collaboration

Multidisciplinary student & faculty teams evaluate challenges and prototype research solutions.

4. Deployment

Industries, startups & CSRs provide funding and mentorship to pilot and scale the solution.
